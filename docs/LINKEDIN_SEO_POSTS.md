What I Learned Building a SaaS in 30 Days 🚀
(Lessons from launching CloudInvoice)

4 weeks ago, I started building CloudInvoice with a simple goal: make GST invoicing stupid simple for Indian businesses.

Here's what I learned:

💡 LESSON 1: Ship Fast, Iterate Faster
• Built MVP in 2 weeks (not 6 months)
• Launched with 70% of planned features
• Users told me what actually mattered
• Shipped 15 updates based on feedback

🎯 Key takeaway: Perfect is the enemy of shipped.

💡 LESSON 2: Solve ONE Problem Really Well
• Initially wanted to build "full accounting software"
• Users just wanted: "Make invoicing not suck"
• Focused 100% on invoicing + payment collection
• Result: Better product, happier users

🎯 Key takeaway: Do one thing exceptionally vs many things poorly.

💡 LESSON 3: Free Removes ALL Friction
• Initially planned ₹499/month pricing
• Made it free to start, paid for advanced features
• 10x more sign-ups
• Users upgrade when they see value

🎯 Key takeaway: Let them taste before buying.

💡 LESSON 4: Build for India FIRST
• GST compliance isn't optional
• ₹ currency and formatting matters
• Razorpay + UPI = must-haves
• "Made for India" = competitive advantage

🎯 Key takeaway: Local context = product-market fit.

💡 LESSON 5: Listen to Users (But Filter Wisely)
• Got 100+ feature requests in week 1
• 80% wanted the same 3 things
• Built those first (invoice tracking, payment reminders, templates)
• Ignored nice-to-haves

🎯 Key takeaway: Prioritize signal over noise.

💡 LESSON 6: Marketing is Distribution, Not Advertising
• Spent ₹0 on ads
• 100% growth from:
  → Product Hunt launch
  → LinkedIn posts (like this!)
  → User referrals
  → Helping people in Slack/WhatsApp groups

🎯 Key takeaway: Be genuinely helpful = best marketing.

💡 LESSON 7: Technical Decisions Matter
• Chose: Next.js + PostgreSQL + AWS
• Could have used: WordPress + plugins
• Trade-off: More complex, but infinitely scalable
• Worth it for long-term

🎯 Key takeaway: Choose tech for where you're going, not where you are.
